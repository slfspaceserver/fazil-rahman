The Chairman

A Legacy Woven in Values

As Director of Classic Group and CEO of Classic Jewels & Diamonds, Fazil Rahman leads a diversified group built on more than 35 years of experience in the gold industry and over 16 years of presence in the UAE. His leadership spans bullion trading, luxury jewellery, and real estate.

Built on tradition, craftsmanship, integrity, and innovation, Classic Group continues to expand its presence across sectors while delivering high-quality products, services, and investment opportunities to clients globally.

Classic Group

Three Divisions. One Legacy.

A diversified business group built around specialised expertise and long-term value creation.

Classic Bullion

Operating as White Classic Gold & Diamonds Trading L.L.C., Classic Bullion is based in Dubai's Gold Souq and specializes in wholesale trading, export, import, and manufacturing of precious metals.

Classic Jewels & Diamonds

Launched in 2024, this division brings Classic Group's precious-metals expertise into fine jewellery, combining traditional craftsmanship with contemporary design.

Classic Realty

Launched in 2022, Classic Realty focuses on high-end real estate investment opportunities across Dubai, serving investors in one of the world's most sought-after property markets.
